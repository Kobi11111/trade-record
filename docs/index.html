How to verify

  1. At 06:35 and 12:10 (Los Angeles) the picks, their publish time and a random nonce are hashed with SHA-256. Only the hash is pushed to the public repo, before the picks are sent anywhere. The commit link next to each slot shows when.
  2. After the close, the exact hashed text is published as reveals/<date>_<slot>.json.
  3. Check it yourself: sha256sum reveals/<date>_<slot>.json (macOS: shasum -a 256) must print the same hash. Or tap Verify next to a slot: your browser downloads the file from GitHub and hashes it locally.
  4. Closed results are appended to trades/*.jsonl and are never edited or deleted. The git history shows every change.

R = result divided by the planned risk (entry to stop). +1R means the trade made what it risked.

📏 How we measure

  1. What is in the record. Only the daily picks published at 06:35 and 12:10 (Los Angeles): the top picks the desk chooses from all its sources, its lab strategies included. No personal trades. A ticker counts once per day. The paper books table below is separate.
  2. When a pick counts. Only if the price touched the entry, counted from the first 5-minute bar after the bar that contains the publish time (06:35 or 12:10) until the close of the next trading day. The publish bar itself never counts. No touch = not triggered: it is shown, but it is in no statistic.
  3. Fill. At the entry price, or at the bar's open when it opened past the entry.
  4. How it ends. Bar by bar, whichever comes first: the stop = a loss, the target = a win. Entry and stop in the same 5-minute bar = a loss. Stop and target in the same bar = the stop (the cautious reading). A bar that opens through the stop = out at that open, which can be worse than −1R. Neither after 20 trading days = out at that day's last close.
  5. Prices. 5-minute bars of the regular session (09:30–16:00 New York) from Alpaca's SIP feed, i.e. the whole US market, unadjusted, read once they are at least 15 minutes old. Results are simulated from these bars, not from real fills.
  6. R. (exit − fill) ÷ (fill − stop); mirrored for a short.
  7. The six numbers. Closed trades: triggered picks that ended. Win rate: the share of them with R above 0. Average R: their mean. Total R: their sum. Max drawdown: the largest fall of the running total R from its highest point, trades in the order they closed. Open now: triggered and not ended yet.
  8. Rules final as of 2026-10-06, before the first pick was committed (the first commit is 2026-10-07 06:35 LA). A change would be listed here with its date and would never be applied to past results.
